# ClairLibre Ideas for Enshrouded

> Eight ClairLibre project concepts, not existing resources yet. Validate game data and technical feasibility before implementation.

1. **Base Planner** - Plan a base layout, rooms, building blocks, and materials before building in game.
1. **Resource & Crafting Calculator** - Calculate the full ingredient chain, stations, and quantities for an item or building project, with an option to subtract materials already stored.
1. **Embervale Companion App** - Keep quests, collections, personal notes, and custom map markers together in searchable checklists.
1. **Vertical Map / Altimeter** - Add elevation readouts, terrain layers, height-aware markers, and route or base planning for Embervale.
1. **Materials & Products Graph** - Explore dependencies among raw materials, products, recipes, stations, and final items in a Cytoscape-style graph.
1. **Enemy & Damage Catalogue** - Compare enemy types by resistance or susceptibility to damage types, with filters for family, region, and damage source.
1. **Sparkworks Blockly Editor** - Design and share Sparkworks circuits with visual Blockly blocks, mapped to in-game nodes where feasible.
1. **Synchronisor** - Multilingual mod or companion: v0 build and maintain an ID-linked EN-to-other-languages dictionary; v1 look up terms in a text field with up to three configurable languages; v2 add spoken translations through real-time translation and text-to-speech services, using game text where available.
